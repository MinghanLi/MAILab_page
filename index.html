[New Paper] OmniCompass: A holistic benchmark for omni-modal video grounding and understanding

OmniCompass evaluates how multimodal models understand both visual and audio-visual evidence in videos. It brings temporal grounding, spatial grounding, and question answering into one benchmark to test when, where, and what happens in a video.
